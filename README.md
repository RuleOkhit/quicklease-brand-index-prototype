# QuickLease Brand Index — prototype

A clickable front-end prototype of the Brand Index: one score for any retail or F&B brand, built to support mall leasing decisions.

**Live page:** https://ruleokhit.github.io/quicklease-brand-index-prototype/

Everything is in `index.html`, a single self-contained file with no build step and no backend.

> **All brands, companies, malls and numbers are fictional.** They are placeholders that show how the app would present real research. None of it describes a real business.

## Screens

- **Home**: search any brand, alerts on your tenants, rising brands, and a check for a broker's list
- **Brand page**: score, pillars, red flags, trend against the category, stores, customers, financials, score maths and sources
- **Compare**: up to three brands side by side
- **Discover**: rankings, young brands, and buzz against fundamentals
- **Fill a slot**: a shortlist for an empty unit
- **Watchlist**: tenant health and lease renewals
- **Scoring model**: adjustable weights per mall
- **How scores work**: the method, plus a back-test on a chain that closed
